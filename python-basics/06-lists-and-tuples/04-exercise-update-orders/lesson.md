The cafe order board has one typo and an item that sold out. Transfer the playlist edit to a different kind of list; `remove(value)` removes by matching value, not position.

This is a separate exercise. Edit `main.py`, press **Run** to inspect the output, then **Submit** to check the numbered tasks.

The starting board is supplied. Update it, then Run and Submit.

## Your Tasks

1. Replace `cofee` at index 1 with `coffee`.
2. Append `water` to `orders`.
3. Remove `cake` from `orders` by value.
