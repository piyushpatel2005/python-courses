# Exercise: Local Scope

In the demo an inner name did not modify the outer name. A shop has a regular price; for one calculation, use a local discount without rewriting the regular price. The starter prints both values.

## Your Tasks

1. Complete `sale_price()` by creating a local `price` equal to `15` and returning it; the outside `price` must remain `20`.

Edit `main.py`, press **Run** to inspect the result, then **Submit** to check your change.
