`for marker in range(1, 4)` visits 1, 2, and 3: the stop value is excluded. `range(4)` starts at zero; `range(2, 8, 2)` visits 2, 4, and 6. A `for` loop advances automatically, unlike a counter-driven `while`. Change `range(1, 4)` to `range(1, 5)` in **main.py**. Press **Run** to see `Marker 4 | ` added, then **Submit**.
