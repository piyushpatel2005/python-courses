The gate needs at least four oil units and at least one key. In the previous lesson, you changed a value and observed the supplied comparisons. Now write each comparison yourself.

`oil_units` and `key_count` are supplied in `main.py`. Replace the two `False` placeholders with expressions that use those variables; a fixed `True` answer would not work if a supply changed. The print calls show your results on separate lines.

## Your Tasks

1. Set `enough_oil` by checking whether `oil_units` is at least `4`.
2. Set `has_key` by checking whether `key_count` is at least `1`.

Press **Run** to see `True` twice, then **Submit**. At the boundary, exactly four units of oil or one key should still count as enough. In the next lesson, you will combine checks like these with logical operators.
