A small grocery store wants at least four bags of flour and one carton of milk on its shelves. In `main.py`, `flour_bags` and `milk_cartons` hold the current quantities. Replace each `False` placeholder with a comparison using those variables; a fixed answer won't respond when the stock changes.

## Your Tasks

1. Set `enough_flour` by checking whether `flour_bags` is at least `4`.
2. Set `has_milk` by checking whether `milk_cartons` is at least `1`.

Press **Run** to see two `True` lines, then **Submit**. Exactly four bags or one carton still meets the minimum. The next lesson combines checks with `and`, `or`, and `not`.
