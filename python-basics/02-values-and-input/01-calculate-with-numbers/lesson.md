A plant stall counts how many trays it can fill. `+`, `-`, `*`, and `/` do addition, subtraction, multiplication, and decimal division. `//` gives whole groups, `%` gives the remainder, and `**` raises to a power. Parentheses make an intended grouping clear: `(2 + 3) * 4` is not `2 + 3 * 4`. Keep numbers unquoted for arithmetic; `/` produces a `float` even with whole-number inputs.

The code calculates whole trays and leftover plants. Change **only** `plants` from `17` to `23`. Edit `main.py`, press **Run** to see `Trays: 5` and `Loose plants: 3`, then **Submit**. The next exercise applies the same operators to another event.

## Your Task

1. Change the number of plants to `23`.
