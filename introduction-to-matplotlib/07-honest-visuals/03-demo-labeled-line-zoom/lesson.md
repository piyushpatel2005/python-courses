# Demo: Label a line zoom

Small changes in garden temperature can matter. Unlike bars, line positions can be zoomed to show detail, provided the title and axis say what the reader is seeing. This chart already has a narrow range; change its title to disclose the detail view. Do not claim that a small change is a dramatic jump.

Edit `main.py`, press **Run** to inspect the figure, then **Submit** to check the change.
